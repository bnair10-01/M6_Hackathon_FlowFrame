# Top 5 Use Cases for AI Smart Glasses

## 1. Real-Time Translation and Captioning
Live subtitles for spoken conversation appear in the wearer's field of view, translated into their language. Travelers, international teams, and people who are deaf or hard of hearing can follow speech without looking down at a phone.

## 2. Hands-Free Guidance for Frontline Work
Technicians, warehouse staff, and field engineers get step-by-step instructions, schematics, and checklists overlaid on the equipment in front of them. A remote expert can see what the wearer sees and annotate it live, which cuts repair times and reduces errors.

## 3. Visual Assistance and Accessibility
For blind and low-vision users, the glasses can describe surroundings, read signs, menus, and documents aloud, recognize faces the user has saved, and help with navigation. This makes daily tasks more independent without needing to hold up a phone.

## 4. Contextual Memory and Personal Assistant
Ask "Where did I leave my keys?" or "What was the name of the person I met at the conference?" With the user's consent, the glasses can recall what they've seen, summarize meetings, surface reminders at the right moment, and answer questions about objects in view.

## 5. Heads-Up Navigation and Information
Turn-by-turn walking or cycling directions, transit times, and points of interest appear in view while the wearer keeps their eyes on the street. The same overlay works for fitness stats, recipe steps while cooking, or speaker notes during a presentation.
